# Privacy Policy for Skynarium

Last updated: October 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Skynarium is a virtual reality astronomy simulator
developed for Meta Quest.

We respect your privacy and are committed to
transparency about how the application processes data.

## 2. Information We Process

Skynarium may process the following information:

- Headset and controller tracking data required
  for virtual reality interactions.
- User-selected settings, including observing
  location, date, time, and display preferences.
- Technical information required for the
  application to operate correctly.

VR tracking data is used to provide the immersive
experience.

Observing locations may be selected manually by
the user.

The application does not require users to create
a separate Skynarium account.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Skynarium is used to:

- Render and operate the VR environment.
- Enable interactions with virtual objects.
- Simulate astronomical observations.
- Apply user-selected application settings.

## 4. Data Storage

Application preferences may be stored locally
on the user's device.

Skynarium does not operate its own cloud-based
user account system.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Skynarium operates on the Meta Quest platform.

Meta may process information independently
according to its own privacy policies.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can remove locally stored Skynarium data
through the device's application storage settings,
where supported.

Users may also contact the developer to request
information about or deletion of any personal
data held by the developer.

Requests can be submitted to:

piratisrael@gmail.com

If no personal data is held by the developer,
we will explain this in response to the request.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Skynarium is not intended to collect personal
information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the
application evolves.

Updates will be published on this page.

## 9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion
requests, contact:
